Hi, I'm Mike. I co-host the Explicit Measures podcast with Tommy Puglia here at PowerBI.tips. And I would like to formally apologize to the English language, because on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, I invented a word by accident, live, on air. That word is **Deplavity**.

This post explains the joke, shows you exactly where it happened, and rounds up every single thing we've made because of it. Videos, the ad, the jingle, all of it. Grab a coffee. Take it once daily.

## The slip

We were recording [episode 573, Fabric Deployment Plan](/2026/10/20/fabric-deployment-plan-ep-573/). Tommy and I were digging into the new deployment plan feature in Microsoft Fabric, and I was making a perfectly reasonable point about how people coming from the Power BI world are getting more comfortable with deployments. I was trying to say "deployment savvy."

My mouth had other plans. Two words went in, one word came out. Here's how I explained myself right after:

> "Deployment savvy was what I meant to say. Like two words instead of just one."
>
> Me, Explicit Measures episode 573, trying to recover

Too late. Tommy immediately told me I had to trademark it before Microsoft took it, and he had a theory about why:

> "They love nothing more than taking two giant words and just compound making a really bad name for the new product."
>
> Tommy, Explicit Measures episode 573

Honestly? Fair. And that's the heart of the joke. Deplavity sounds exactly like something that could show up in a product announcement. It also sounds exactly like something you'd hear at the end of a TV commercial, right before a very calm voice reads you forty side effects.

## Then it became a prescription drug

By the end of the episode, the word had already grown legs. I have to give credit to John for this one, because the idea that Deplavity sounds like a prescription drug was his. As I put it on the show:

> "Deplavity sounds like a prescription drug to solve your deployment fabric artifacts."
>
> Me, Explicit Measures episode 573

So of course I read Tommy a full pharma ad, live, side effects and all. "Ask your capacity admin about deplavity." Not approved for Premium Per User. Then we wrapped the show with Deplavity as our sponsor. Our first sponsor ever, and it doesn't exist. That feels right for us.

If you have ever deployed a semantic model to Prod and watched it keep pointing at Dev, you already understand why this landed. Deplavity is a love letter to every Fabric and Power BI person fighting Chronic Deployment Dysfunction and Artifact Drift. It's a joke because it's true.
